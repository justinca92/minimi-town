Minimi Town.

Your Steam Deck library as a little street. Every game is a storefront, and a baby penguin walks to whichever one you pick. This is the plugin's own street running in your browser, with made-up games.

Download for Decky GitHub

On the Deck, Steam's own game page opens here (Play, Install, achievements). Come back and the penguin steps out of the same door.

Play with it

◀ ▶
Move along the street (or swipe)
Enter
Ⓐ: the penguin walks in and the game's page opens
X
Ⓧ: the screen turns round to show playtime, achievements, friends
Y
Ⓨ: shake the street
Mouse
Click to poke the penguin, rub to pet it, drag and fling to throw it. Poke it too often and it gets cross.
Deck
The left trackpad is a hand: rub to pet, quick click to poke, hold to pick up, flick to throw.
